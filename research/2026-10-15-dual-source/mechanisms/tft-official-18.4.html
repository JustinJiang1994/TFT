Teamfight Tactics patch 18.4

18.4 is about moving power from traits into units, buffing under-performing lines and shipping consistency buffs to our feast or famine champs!
Game Updates
Rodger “Riot Prism” Caudill, Katie "Riot Ukime" Guo

It's a big one!

We’re a couple patches into the set now and most comps/lines are pretty established, so we’re taking a big swing here to enable a deeper diversity of comps and strategies through trait adjustments, buffs for our struggling 1 and 2-costs and a few nerfs to the dominant strategies. Going big here allows us to use 18.5 (next patch) as a more focused, smaller patch, dedicated to smaller tweaks to help us achieve our goals here.

TRAITS

Our aim with traits this patch is to bring Origins and Classes closer together in power. Currently, some deep vertical Origins aren’t meeting the power bar for their commitment, while a few vertical Classes are providing too much combat power, leading to some especially powerful Emblems.

Blossom: Blossom has proven to be a great splash with the value of upgraded Wisps, but the deeper verticals for the trait have yet to be fully explored, so we’re adding more stats to see what Blossom (7) and even (9) can bloom into.

Coven: Coven’s Stage 2/3 cashout at 130 essence is too consistent and safe for a cashout trait. We’re lowering the rewards here since it’s outperforming the other ways to play the trait.

Eclipse: For a (somewhat) hidden trait, Eclipse is supposed to be powerful, but it arrives too late, both in the calendar (with February 6th being our next solar eclipse), and in the fight itself, especially when the first few executes on the lowest health enemies tend to lack impact.

Elderwood: Elderwood (9) is pretty good, but Elderwood (7) is currently tied to Emblems to create powerful boards. With Emblem-linked strategies coming down this patch, and with Elderwood (7)’s lackluster performance already, we’re shipping a solid buff to the Deepwood Protector, that alongside a Elderwood (9) nerf should be close to neutral (but still a nerf) at Elderwood (9).

Fae: Fae is very dependent on an Emblem, so we’re taking some power away from the trait (and thus the Emblem) to budget more into three buffs for the Fae units.

Juggernaut: Juggernaut (6) is currently too easy to hit, play, and flex around, providing a frontline backbone that’s just too far over its power budget for how easy it is to slot in.

Lunar: No one is chasing Lunar, not even Warwick and Murkwolf (note this is a joke about wolves chasing the moon, not a tip to play Lunar vertical with Warwick and Murkwold). We’re adding a bigger AP incentive for going deep in the trait, which should also make Lunar Lux much more appealing.

Sprkyin: For a trait that needs an Emblem and a 5-cost to hit, Sprykin (7) is pretty weak. We’re giving the BFF buff even more HP and AS, but as a reminder, this helps ALL your Sprkyins because at (7) the BFF applies to all of them.

UNITS: TIER 1

Our 1 and 2-cost changes are mostly buffs, allowing more reroll lines to appear in the meta.

Camille: Camille has been conditional since her nerf last patch, here we’re just buffing her three and four-star forms so when you do hit, she hits harder too!

Kobuko: Kobuko sees play on a few boards, but most of those are Blackthorn boards where our favorite Yordle is sacrificed for tank stats. A better AP ratio may finally have him itemized and NOT just for the Blackthorn Item Extraction Augment!

Xayah and Rakan: Birds of a feather get buffed together! Well, not all the time, but in this case we’re buffing both Xayah and Rakan to help give you a reason to itemize Rakan and to bring back their Solar reroll line that hasn’t been seen in a few patches. Both buffs here are to their AD/AP ratios which are amped a lot by the stats Fae provides.

Varus: Varus has been hurting for this full set. He’s pretty close, so we’re upping his Attack Speed so he can get to casting more often—something that should help his consistency since he occasionally misses his big boom cast.

Veigar: Veigar is currently the best performing 1-cost reroll line—perhaps the only one truly successful in 18.3. With the nerfs here, we still think he will succeed with strong starts, and specific Artifacts, but it should be a taller order than before…. Hehe.

Kayle and Shen: Kayle and Shen are potentially our fourth and fifth Solar reroll unit buffs (after Xayah, Rakan, and Varus as 1-costs). They're getting smaller buffs to enable Solar lines (which have been almost completely missing last patch), and other low-cost reroll lines.

UNITS: TIER 4

Brambleback: Brambleback is really inconsistent, relying on his leap between targets to take out enemies in the backline. We’re moving some of this power into his consistent cast for those situations where he gets stuck on that super-Maokai. Overall, this should be a buff to Brambleback.

Ezreal: With Executioner as a trait coming down in power, we’re able to give Ezreal a small buff, as his success is currently too tied to Executioner Emblem duo (or triple) carries.

Nidalee: With an AP buff to Nidalee we’re hoping to add one more AP carry line for fast 8/9 strategies. Alongside that, we have a small nerf to AD Nidalee that tends to take over fights in the mid-game when she can clean up backliners more efficiently.

UNITS: TIER 5

Ashe and Draven: We’ve got two quality of Life changes for our 5-costs that can (and rarely) whiff their ability. When Ashe and Draven do miss, they will refund some of their Mana to cast sooner. While having a small chance to miss abilities is a mechanic to allow for more interesting fights and powerful highs (when on the other side of the whiff) in an ability, we want to make sure our 5-costs aren’t subject to that inconsistency in the same way that 1-cost (e.g. Varus) are.

Ashe: Ashe is already one of our better 5-costs even before giving her a consistency buff, so we’re also giving her a nerf that mainly targets her unitemized form.

Draven: Since we’ve nerfed his bounty cashouts, Draven’s been struggling as an end-game carry. We’re giving him a better AD ratio on his bleed to help him cash in.

Ivern: We’re taking a pass at rebalancing Ivern’s hexes. Since Damage Amp is too valuable due to its multiplicative nature with AD/AP and crit, Mushrooms are taking a hit. Meanwhile, the craggy Rock hexes are getting more Armor and Magic Resist to keep them on par with the more powerful forested Tree hexes.

Kennen: Kennen is a terror of a two-star 5-cost, who ramps into the fight as more enemies burn. Since he gets a lot of AP from burning foes, nerfing his shield’s AP ratio should make him an easier flame to put out than before, especially when he becomes the focus of your backline. As a reminder, Edge of Night is and still will be one of his best items.

Lux: Solar Lux rarely makes sense to add to your board, but when it does it’s pretty good. We’re making that niche even stronger by changing her Solar bonus to make her the highest damage dealing Lux variant, so even if you’re not playing Solar reroll, you may still want to pick her up if you have the right items.
