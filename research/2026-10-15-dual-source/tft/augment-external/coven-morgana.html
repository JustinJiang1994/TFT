Coven Morgana: TFT Set 18 comp guide

A tierFast 9MediumLevel 9Patch 18.4b

By PivotTFT · Updated 11 October 2026

Morgana and Alune cast behind Vanguard 4, with Coven 3 from Cassiopeia and Elise, and Elder Dragon's two Riftbeasts plus Sentinel for Riftbeast 3. Pushed to level 9 for its 5-costs, it places well at every rank in 18.4b, and best below Diamond.

Open in builder

Overview

A level 9 line from Coven or Vanguard units: Coven gathers essence from kills and from lost rounds, so it softens a weak start while you save for 9. Alune Fast 9 plays the same Morgana with Hecarim, Lillia and Ivern instead of the Coven units and places higher, so follow the units the shop gives you. Turn Coven's essence into rewards when one helps you right away.

How to play Coven Morgana

Early game

Coven or Vanguard pairs (Elise, Camille, Rakan) and save gold for level 9.

  • Elise
  • Camille
  • Rakan
  • Leona

Mid game

Level 8 at 4-1 for Morgana and Sentinel; Coven's essence turns into rewards as you lose rounds or take kills.

Late game

Level 9 for Alune, Taric and Elder Dragon; the Dragon takes two slots and counts as two Riftbeasts.

TipMorgana takes a Vanguard Emblem, Morellonomicon and Void Staff (without the emblem, Rabadon's Deathcap), Alune Adaptive Helm, Jeweled Gauntlet and Shojin, and Sentinel Gargoyle Stoneplate, Protector's Vow and Warmog's.

Augment priority: econ, then items.

Questions about Coven Morgana

Is Coven Morgana good in patch 18.4b?

Coven Morgana is A tier on the PivotTFT comp tier list for patch 18.4b: a strong comp this patch and often the right call when your early units or items point to it.

Who is the carry in Coven Morgana?

Morgana is the main carry. Build Vanguard Emblem, Morellonomicon and Void Staff on Morgana.

What level do you play Coven Morgana at?

Coven Morgana is a Fast 9 comp: you push to level 9 for 5-cost champions and the biggest late-game board. Roll for the board at level 9.

How do I import Coven Morgana into TFT?

Copy its team code with the button on this page, then open the Team Planner in the TFT client and paste it.

What are the best augments for Coven Morgana?

Coven Acolyte, Staffsmith, Level Up! and Late Game Specialist. When none of these is offered, take econ, then items augments.
